PDF Dude - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Dude ("the App"), developed by N. Muralidharan ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Access

PDF Dude allows users to select and work with PDF files and images on their devices. Files selected by users are accessed only to provide the requested app functionality.

2. Document Processing and Storage

PDF and image processing is performed locally on your device. Your selected documents are not uploaded to our servers as part of the app's document-processing features. Files created or exported by the app are saved to the location selected by the user.

3. Personal Information

The app does not require users to create an account. We do not intentionally collect names, phone numbers, or email addresses through the app.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. We do not share users' documents with third parties through the app's local document-processing features.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

The app's core document-processing functionality is designed to work offline. If future versions introduce third-party services, advertising, analytics, or other online features, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant information practices.

6. Data Security

Documents processed by the app remain under the user's control on their device. Users should protect their devices and maintain backups of important documents.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a central server containing users' documents through the app's local processing features. Users can manage or delete exported files using their device's file manager. App-created temporary files, if any, are handled according to the app's implementation and device storage behaviour.

8. Children's Privacy

PDF Dude is a general-purpose utility application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
